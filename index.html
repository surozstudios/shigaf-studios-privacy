Tower Builder Privacy Policy

Draft — pending verification of the release build.

Shigaf Studios develops Tower Builder. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game. Contact: surozstudios@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Tower Builder saves your best score and sound preferences locally. The game does not require a player account. It also stores a small local diagnostic log to help investigate advertisement loading errors.

Advertisements and third-party services

The game integrates Google AdMob for banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements that allow a player to continue after losing.

Google's advertising SDK may process technical information such as your IP address, approximate location inferred from that address, app and advertisement interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers, depending on its configuration. These data may be used for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's practices.

Children's privacy

Tower Builder is intended for children under 13. The game does not ask players to enter their name, email address, or date of birth. Parents and guardians may contact Shigaf Studios with questions about information associated with their child's use of the game.

Support communications

If a parent or guardian emails us, we receive the sender's email address and the information included in the message. We use this information to respond and resolve the request. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information about a child.

Storage and deletion

Local game data can be removed using Android's clear-storage controls. This resets saved scores and preferences. To request deletion of support correspondence held by us, contact the email address above. Information processed by Google is subject to Google's retention practices and available controls.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game's features or data practices change. The published policy will show its update date.
